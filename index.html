    How to read your time

    Most adults land between 200 and 300 ms on a simple visual test like this one. Your device adds a little: the screen refresh, the mouse or touch screen, and the browser all take a few milliseconds, so compare yourself with yourself, on the same device.

    Sleep, fatigue, caffeine and the time of day all shift the result. The spread often says more than the fastest time: a steady series means your attention held from one trial to the next.

    This test measures a simple reaction time. It is not a diagnosis, and it is not a clinical attention test.
